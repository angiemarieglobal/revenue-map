The one line to hold

Every sale has a doorway before it. Set where each offer's buyers come from and what share of them say yes. The map works out how many people need to arrive at that doorway to hit your sales plan. The close rates loaded here are starting assumptions, so replace them with your real numbers as each launch gives them to you.

Three ways money lands. Single payment lands in full the month of sale. Monthly lands every month the member stays, less the monthly cancel rate. Payment plan lands a deposit the month of sale, then an instalment each month after. Existing members counts people already paying you monthly when the plan starts. A payment plan's contract value is its deposit plus every instalment. A monthly membership has no end date, so choose how many months each new member counts for in contracts signed.
